When prototyping new microcontrollers, cable management on the breadboard is essential for debugging. A chaotic layout leads to logic errors. 

By utilizing pre-cut jumper wires and aligning the ESP32 directly to the power rails, we create a clean, logical pathway for the 3.3V logic flow.
